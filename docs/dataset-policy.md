# Dataset and Licensing Policy

- Use only manuals you are authorized to process. Public download does not imply redistribution rights.
- Do not commit vendor manuals, raw or extracted copyrighted passages, proprietary diagrams, client records, or credentials.
- Store local source PDFs and generated indexes under the git-ignored `data/` directory.
- Make public test fixtures synthetic, with clearly fictional equipment and fault codes.
- Remove confidential identifiers from demos, benchmarks, screenshots and logs.
- Do not send documents or retrieved chunks to external embedding, LLM, OCR or telemetry APIs without permission.
- Inspect staged files before every push; perform secret checks.
